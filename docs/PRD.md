# Leebs — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product

Leebs is a lightweight, mobile-first learning web application
designed initially for Nigerian secondary-school students preparing
for JAMB, WAEC and NECO examinations.

The invention-sprint MVP focuses on JAMB Biology.

## 2. Problem

Many learners can follow classroom instruction but have limited
opportunities for individual explanation when they do not understand
a concept.

Private tutoring and extra lessons may be inaccessible because of
cost, location or other constraints.

Learners may therefore resort to memorising answers without developing
strong conceptual understanding.

Digital learning tools can introduce additional barriers when they
depend on constant connectivity, large downloads or expensive data.

## 3. User

### Primary Persona

Adora is a fictional 16-year-old Nigerian secondary-school student.

She is preparing for JAMB.

She has access to a smartphone but may experience unreliable internet
and needs to be careful about mobile data usage.

She follows lessons at school but sometimes needs additional
explanation and cannot rely on private tutoring.

Adora wants to know:

- What should I study?
- Do I actually understand this?
- Why was my answer wrong?
- Can someone explain this differently?
- Am I improving?

## 4. Product Hypothesis

If learners receive:

- short curriculum-aligned learning activities
- opportunities to explain their reasoning
- immediate formative feedback
- guided explanations when confused
- visible evidence of growing mastery

then they can develop stronger understanding while remaining engaged
in their learning journey.

## 5. Product Differentiator

Leebs should not behave like an answer machine.

Before the AI Coach provides substantial help, learners are encouraged
to demonstrate their current understanding.

When a learner answers incorrectly, Leebs asks:

"Why did you choose that answer?"

The learner's reasoning becomes input to the coaching process.

The AI then helps diagnose the misconception and guides the learner
toward understanding.

## 6. Core Learning Loop

Learn
→ Attempt
→ Reflect
→ AI Coach
→ Prove Understanding
→ Track Progress

## 7. MVP Goals

The MVP must demonstrate that a learner can:

1. identify what to study next
2. complete a micro-lesson
3. attempt an assessment
4. explain their reasoning
5. receive misconception-aware guidance
6. demonstrate understanding on a new question
7. record a reflection
8. see their topic mastery change

## 8. Experience Principles

### Lightweight

Prefer text, simple graphics and short interactions.

### Mobile-first

Design around small smartphone screens.

### Encouraging

Mistakes should feel like part of learning.

### Understanding-first

Completion alone should not imply mastery.

### Locally relevant

Examples should be relatable to Nigerian learners when appropriate.

### Resilient

Core learning should remain useful with unreliable connectivity.

## 9. MVP Success

The invention-sprint MVP succeeds when someone can experience the
complete golden learning path and understand Leebs' value without
requiring an explanation from the builder.
